The problem

Production and demand rarely meet in time.

Across Sub-Saharan Africa, smallholders often plant without clear visibility of what buyers will need. Hotels, markets, and institutions struggle with inconsistent local supply. The result is post-harvest waste, unstable farmer incomes, and missed opportunities for domestic and regional food systems.

Blind planting

Farmers produce without advance signals from hotels, institutions, or retailers — leading to surplus or the wrong mix of crops.

Unpredictable supply

Buyers face last-minute shortages, variable quality, and heavy reliance on intermediaries with little planning data.

No shared view

There is limited coordination between production and market demand — and almost no aggregated data for local planning.

How it works

Coordinate. Match. Add trust on the order.

Register as a farmer or buyer, post availability or demand, review match opportunities, then open a trust / order record for origin, quality photos, and payment proof — mobile-first and offline-capable.

  1. 01

    Choose your role

    Open LakazAgri and select I am a Farmer or I am a Buyer. You can change roles later in settings.

  2. 02

    Create a simple profile

    Register with full name, phone number, location (district/village/region), and optional organisation or farm name. Privacy-first by design.

  3. 03

    Post availability or demand

    Farmers share crop, quantity, location, and date available. Buyers post product needed, quantity, deadline, and preferred area or market.

  4. 04

    Browse matches

    See supply–demand opportunities, open a match, and contact the other party (e.g. WhatsApp) when you are ready to deal.

  5. 05

    Trust on the order

    Create a batch ID with origin details, add quality photos and notes, track payment status and attach receipt proof — all offline-first.

What’s in the pilot

Coordination plus trust on every matched order.

For farmers

Post what you have. See what buyers need.

  • Register as a farmer with location and farm details
  • Post product availability with quantity and date
  • Browse matches and open a trust / order record
  • Record origin zone, variety, harvest date → batch ID
  • Add quality photos (up to 3) and notes offline
For buyers

Signal demand early. Secure reliable local supply.

  • Register as a hotel, market, restaurant, trader, or institution
  • Post advance demand with needed-by dates
  • See matches and open the same trust / order record
  • View origin summary and shared quality photo log
  • Track payment status and attach receipt proof

Why it matters

Visibility first. Then fewer disputes.

Coordination solves the planning bottleneck. The trust layer then reduces fights over origin, quality, and payment — using a simple batch ID and shared offline records, without requiring blockchain for day-to-day use.

  • Plan ahead Advance demand signals for hotels, markets, traders and institutions
  • Cut waste Less surplus planting and fewer last-minute procurement surprises
  • Local first Supports food security and stronger domestic and regional sourcing
  • Fewer disputes Shared origin, quality photos, and payment proof on each matched order
